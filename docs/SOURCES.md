# Sources

All sources are public. Formulas are standard; framing, worked examples and code are the author's. Where an edition is uncertain it is marked.

## Verification, validation and test management

- ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023, *Systems and software engineering: System life cycle processes* (verification and validation processes).
- NASA, *Systems Engineering Handbook*, NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev 2, 2016, sections 5.3 and 5.4.
- US Department of Defense, DoDI 5000.89, *Test and Evaluation*, 2020 (developmental and operational test roles).
- US Department of Defense Directive 3000.09, *Autonomy in Weapon Systems*, 2023 (test and evaluation against adaptive adversaries).
- UK Ministry of Defence, Development, Concepts and Doctrine Centre, *Red Teaming Handbook*, 3rd ed., 2021.
- Montgomery, D. C., *Design and Analysis of Experiments*, Wiley, 10th ed. (factorial test matrices).

## Technology readiness

- Mankins, J. C., *Technology Readiness Levels: A White Paper*, NASA Office of Space Access and Technology, 1995.
- European Commission, Horizon 2020 Work Programme 2014-2015, General Annex G, *Technology readiness levels*.
- US Government Accountability Office, GAO-20-48G, *Technology Readiness Assessment Guide*, 2020.
- US Department of Defense, *Manufacturing Readiness Level Deskbook* (MRL definitions).

## Reliability demonstration and intervals

- NIST/SEMATECH, *e-Handbook of Statistical Methods*, sections 7.2 (proportions) and 8.3 (reliability demonstration). https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/
- Wilson, E. B., "Probable Inference, the Law of Succession, and Statistical Inference", *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 22 (1927): 209-212.
- Clopper, C. J. and Pearson, E. S., "The Use of Confidence or Fiducial Limits Illustrated in the Case of the Binomial", *Biometrika* 26 (1934): 404-413.
- Brown, L. D., Cai, T. T. and DasGupta, A., "Interval Estimation for a Binomial Proportion", *Statistical Science* 16 (2001): 101-133.
- Hanley, J. A. and Lippman-Hand, A., "If Nothing Goes Wrong, Is Everything All Right? Interpreting Zero Numerators", *JAMA* 249 (1983): 1743-1745.
- ISO 5725-1, *Accuracy (trueness and precision) of measurement methods and results*.

## Detection statistics and base rates

- Richards, M. A., *Fundamentals of Radar Signal Processing*, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill, 2014 (Pd, Pfa, thresholding).
- Fawcett, T., "An Introduction to ROC Analysis", *Pattern Recognition Letters* 27 (2006): 861-874.
- Swets, J. A., *Signal Detection Theory and ROC Analysis in Psychology and Diagnostics*, Lawrence Erlbaum, 1996.
- Kahneman, D. and Tversky, A., "On the Psychology of Prediction", *Psychological Review* 80 (1973): 237-251 (base-rate neglect).
- Everingham, M. et al., "The PASCAL Visual Object Classes (VOC) Challenge", *International Journal of Computer Vision* 88 (2010): 303-338 (detector precision and recall).

## Accuracy statistics

- US Department of Defense, *Global Positioning System Standard Positioning Service Performance Standard*, 5th ed., 2020 (accuracy statistic conventions).
- Groves, P. D., *Principles of GNSS, Inertial, and Multisensor Integrated Navigation Systems*, 2nd ed., Artech House, 2013.

## What is not sourced

The TRL "evidence to exit" column, the claim-verdict rule in `tevv.check_claim`, the synthetic error sample in `examples/`, and all worked examples are the author's synthesis for teaching. They are not an official assessment method.
